# RSS 2027 — Stage 1 (Initial Submission) guidelines

- Authors submit a **6-page Extended Abstract: up to 5 pages of content and 1 page of references**.
  Hard limit. Template: IEEEtran conference, two columns, natbib + plainnat (provided template.tex).
  Anonymous review ("Author Names Omitted for Anonymous Review").
- Stage 1 is a semi-complete paper: the **problem statement, hypotheses and methodology must be fully
  fleshed out**; experiments or empirical validation may be missing or preliminary. The core must lay out
  the proposed methods and **detail the insights to be gained, even in the event of negative results**.
- Due to the strict content limit, the **related work should cite only literature directly tied to the
  paper's central problem and novelty**; reviewers evaluate citations on direct relevance, not
  comprehensiveness. A complete related work comes with the Stage 2 final submission.
- Anything beyond the 6 pages (websites, appendices) is not reviewed. **No appendix.**
- Sizing: one IEEEtran page ≈ 1,000–1,100 words of text without floats. Floats available (all wide):
  fig_designs_q150.png (7.16 in × ~1.3 in), study0_pole_sketch.png (~1.7 in at 0.9 textwidth),
  fig_study1_cpad.png (~1.8 in), fig_study2_q120.png (~1.8 in). A column table of ~10 rows ≈ 2 in.
  Content budget therefore ≈ 3,600–3,900 words including equations.
