   Abstract—Research hands for dexterous manipulation are
built as chains of servos, one at each joint. Such hands leave
room for a contact interface only at the fingertip, and a finger
redesigned to carry a thicker interface changes with it, so
the effect of the interface itself cannot be measured. This
paper proposes contact-centric design, a finger built around
its contact interfaces, which moves the servo off the joint
and redesigns the finger for a new interface by preserving the
motion of its contact surfaces, dispensing with the skeleton
