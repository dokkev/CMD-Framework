        CoDe Hand: Room for Contact Interfaces and
         Redesigns That Keep the Contact Jacobian

   Abstract—Contact interfaces such as thick compliant pads
and tactile modules decide how a robot hand touches an object.
Research hands built as chains of servos leave room for an
interface only at the fingertip, and a finger redesigned to carry
a thicker one changes with it, so a measured difference cannot
be attributed to the interface alone. First, a finger with remote
actuation and a crossed four-bar joint keeps more contact
surface than a servo-at-joint finger at every pad thickness and
required flexion. Second, a redesign should keep the finger’s
contact Jacobian, how its contact surfaces move relative to
each other, not its skeleton. A fixed axis cannot, with a closed-
