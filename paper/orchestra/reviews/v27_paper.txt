   Abstract—Contact interfaces such as thick compliant pads
and tactile modules decide how a robot hand touches an
object. Many research hands are chains of servos at the joints,
which leave room for an interface only at the fingertip: a
proximal pad must be thin or cost range of motion. When
the finger is redesigned to carry a thicker interface, the hand                           Figure 1 (placeholder)

finger with remote actuation and a crossed four-bar joint keeps
more contact surface than a servo-at-joint finger at every pad
thickness and required flexion. Second, a redesigned finger
should keep its contact Jacobian, how its contact surfaces
