# HowTO Research Paper Guidelines (Animesh Garg)

Reference for structuring the paper; transcribed from the original image
[howto_research_paper_guidelines_garg.png](howto_research_paper_guidelines_garg.png).
"Surprisingly, the same outline also works for project planning and presentations."

- **Problem Statement**
  - This needs to clearly answer "What". Should be easy to understand for a broad audience in the area.
  - Clearly outline input/output.
  - In the paper you will need one figure to clarify this. This would be the "Money Figure" aka Fig 1.
- **Motivation and Impact**
  - This should answer the "Why"? Why do this. Is there a need or a potential paradigm shift.
  - And more importantly the "So What"? What changes if your proposed solution actually works.
- **Prior Work and State of the Art**
  - List key technical challenges of the problem, with how previous work addresses them.
  - Any other line of work that serves as inspiration for your solution.
  - Explicitly state what the SoTA does not do, and what key technical gap remains.
- **Our Intuition**
  - This should answer the "How" question?
  - Sometimes this can be included in the Fig 1.
- **Our Key Contributions**
  - This is a punchline summary. If this requires you to explain in nuances, rather than crisp outcome, then it needs work!
- **Technical Details**
  - Describe the approach in detail for comprehension.
  - Should mainly try to focus the key technical innovation, while presenting other material for completeness/comprehension.
  - In the paper you will need 1–2 figures to clarify this. This would be the "method" aka Fig 2 and Fig 3.
- **Experimental Setup & Metrics**
  - What is a good setup to evaluate your hypothesis, and what is an independent metric.
  - What data, other code, simulation will you use.
  - In empirical papers this would require figures to illustrate setup. If constrained for space put this figure in appendix but don't omit it.
- **Experiments**
  - Your super cool results. With both qualitative and quantitative results.
